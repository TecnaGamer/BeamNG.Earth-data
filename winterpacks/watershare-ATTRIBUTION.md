# watershare: share of surface water (BeamNG.Earth)

Derived from the **JRC Global Surface Water** dataset, version 1.5 (1984-2024), its occurrence layer (30 m), condensed
to 0.004 degrees by BeamNG.Earth-data's `tools/build_water_pack.py`: per cell the mean occurrence of water (the expected
share of water on a day of 1984-2024), pixels with no data (the open sea beyond the mapped coast) counted as water.

Source: EC JRC/Google. Jean-Francois Pekel, Andrew Cottam, Noel Gorelick, Alan S. Belward (2016): High-resolution
mapping of global surface water and its long-term changes. Nature 540, 418-422. doi:10.1038/nature20584.
https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/
